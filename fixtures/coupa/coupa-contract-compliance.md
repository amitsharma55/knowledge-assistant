# Coupa Contract Compliance

Measures how much spend flows through negotiated contracts versus off-contract,
so leakage can be found and closed.

## Metric

- Contract compliance is the share of addressable spend transacted against an
  active contract.
- Off-contract spend above a threshold on a contracted commodity raises a
  leakage alert to the category manager.
