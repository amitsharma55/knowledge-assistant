# Coupa Supplier Diversity

Tracks supplier diversity classifications so spend with diverse suppliers can be
reported.

## Classification source

- Diversity classifications are sourced from a third-party certification
  provider, not self-attested, and refreshed monthly.
- A lapsed certification drops the supplier's diverse status until recertified.

## Reporting

Diverse spend is reported by classification category against total addressable
spend.
