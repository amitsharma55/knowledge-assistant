# Coupa Middleware Workgroup Directory

The support workgroup, single point of contact (SPOC), and how to reach the team
that owns the Coupa AWS Middleware integrations. Use this when you need to raise
or route a ticket to the right group.

## Workgroup and contacts

- **Workgroup ID:** WG1111
- **Workgroup name:** Coupa AWS Middleware Support
- **Team:** Coupa AWS Middleware (Middleware Platform team)
- **SPOC (single point of contact):** Priya Nair
- **SPOC email:** priya.nair@example.com
- **Support channel:** `#coupa-middleware`
- **Support hours:** Monday–Friday, 08:00–18:00 CT
- **Paging:** PagerDuty service `coupa-aws-middleware` for after-hours incidents

## Ticket routing

- Assign incidents and requests for Coupa middleware integrations to workgroup
  **WG1111**.
- The SPOC, Priya Nair, is the first contact for status, prioritization, and
  escalation of anything assigned to WG1111.
- Business-hours questions go to `#coupa-middleware`; genuine outages page the
  PagerDuty rotation.
