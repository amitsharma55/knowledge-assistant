# Coupa Audit Trail

How changes to key Coupa records are logged for audit, and how long the trail
is retained.

## Coverage

- Approvals, supplier bank-detail changes, and PO edits are logged with actor
  and timestamp.

## Retention

Audit records are retained seven years and are read-only once written.
