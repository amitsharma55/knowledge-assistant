# Coupa Mobile Approvals

The Coupa mobile app lets approvers act on requisitions and invoices away from a
desk.

## Capabilities

- Approvers can approve, reject, or add a comment from the mobile app.
- Approvals made offline are queued on the device and submitted when
  connectivity returns.

## Limits

Editing a requisition's lines is not supported on mobile; that requires the web
application.
