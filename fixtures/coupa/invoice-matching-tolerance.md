# Invoice Matching Tolerance

The tolerances that govern two- and three-way match before an invoice can pay.
This is match configuration, not the AVR read path.

## Tolerances

- Price variance up to 2% or $25, whichever is lower, auto-matches.
- Quantity over-receipt beyond tolerance holds the invoice for review.
