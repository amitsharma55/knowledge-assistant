# Contract Clause Library

The library of approved standard clauses available when authoring a contract in
Icertis. Reference content, not an integration.

## Structure

- Clauses are grouped by type and carry an approval status and an owning legal
  reviewer.
- Only approved clauses may be inserted without a legal review step.
