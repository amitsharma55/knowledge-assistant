# 401(k) Plan

The company retirement savings plan, its match, and vesting.

## Match

- The company matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible pay contributed each pay
  period.

## Vesting

- Employee contributions are always fully vested.
- The company match vests on a three-year cliff schedule from hire date.
