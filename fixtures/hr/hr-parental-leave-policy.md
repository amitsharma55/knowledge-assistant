# Parental Leave Policy

Paid leave for the birth or adoption of a child, separate from annual paid time
off.

## Benefit

- Twelve weeks of paid parental leave for the primary caregiver.
- Leave may be taken continuously or intermittently within the first year.

## Requesting

Requested in Workday as a leave of absence; it does not draw down the annual
time-off balance.
