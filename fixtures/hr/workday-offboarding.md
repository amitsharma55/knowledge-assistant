# Offboarding

How terminations are processed in Workday and what downstream actions they
trigger.

## Steps

- The manager initiates the termination with a last working day.
- On that date, access is revoked through the badge and security-role feeds.

## Final pay

Final pay, including any accrued time-off payout, is calculated in the next
scheduled pay cycle after the termination is approved.
