# Headcount Reporting

How headcount and position data are reported out of Workday for finance and
planning. This is the reporting layer over the organization data.

## Reports

- Filled versus open positions by cost center, refreshed nightly.
- Attrition trailing twelve months by organization.

## Notes

Reports read the position and worker records; they do not change the reporting
hierarchy, which is maintained elsewhere.
