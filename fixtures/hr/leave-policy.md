# Leave Policy

Annual leave, carry-over and the other leave types tracked in Workday. Balances
are held in Workday and accrue automatically; nothing here is calculated by
hand.

## Annual leave

Employees accrue **25 days** of annual leave per calendar year, accrued
**monthly** at 2.08 days per month. Accrual begins on the first day of the
month following the start date.

- Part-time employees accrue pro rata on contracted hours
- Accrual continues during paid leave and during the first 26 weeks of
  parental leave
- Accrual stops during unpaid leave of absence and resumes on return
- Leave may be taken before it is accrued, up to five days, with manager
  approval

## Carry-over

- Up to **five days** may be carried into the next calendar year
- Carried days expire on **March 31** and are not paid out if unused
- Carry-over above five days needs HR business partner approval and is granted
  only where leave could not be taken for business reasons
- On termination, accrued and untaken leave is paid out; leave taken but not
  yet accrued is deducted from the final payment

## Other leave types

- **Sick leave** — no fixed annual entitlement; recorded in Workday and
  reviewed against the absence policy after ten cumulative days in a rolling
  twelve months
- **Parental leave** — separate entitlement, not deducted from annual leave
- **Bereavement leave** — up to five days per event for an immediate family
  member
- **Jury service** — paid in full for the duration, recorded separately
- **Unpaid leave of absence** — by agreement, and pauses annual leave accrual

## Requesting and approving leave

- Requests are raised in Workday and route to the manager of the worker's
  supervisory organization
- A request that would take the balance negative beyond the five-day allowance
  is blocked by Workday rather than routed
- Requests longer than ten consecutive working days additionally route to the
  next manager up
- There is no minimum notice period in policy, though teams may set their own

## Escalation

Disputes about leave balances go first to the manager, then to the HR business
partner for the employee's division, and then to the Head of People. Balance
disputes are almost always an accrual start-date question, so check the accrual
start date before escalating.
