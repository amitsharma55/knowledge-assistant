# Sick Leave Policy

Paid sick time, how it accrues, and how it is used.

## Accrual

- Sick time accrues each pay period up to an annual cap and carries over one
  year.

## Use

Sick time covers the employee's own illness and caring for an immediate family
member; it is recorded in Workday as a sick absence.
