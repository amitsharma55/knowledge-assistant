# Time Tracking

How hourly workers record time in Workday and how it flows to pay. Salaried
exempt employees do not track time here.

## Entry

- Time is entered daily and submitted weekly for manager approval.
- Overtime above 40 hours in a week is calculated automatically at the
  applicable multiplier.

## Downstream

Approved time is picked up by the pay calculation each cycle. Time entered
after the cycle cutoff is carried into the following period as a retro
adjustment.
