# Learning

How training and compliance courses are assigned and tracked in Workday
Learning.

## Assignments

- Compliance courses are auto-assigned by job profile and have a due date 30
  days from assignment.
- Managers can assign optional courses to their teams at any time.

## Completion

Completions are recorded against the worker's learning transcript. Overdue
compliance courses appear on the manager's dashboard until cleared.
