# Onboarding

The tasks a new hire and their manager complete in Workday before and during
the first week.

## Pre-start

- The offer accept creates the worker record and triggers IT provisioning.
- I-9 and tax forms are assigned as onboarding tasks due by day one.

## First week

Benefits enrollment opens as a new-hire life event. Required compliance courses
are auto-assigned with a 30-day due date.
