# Tuition Reimbursement

The benefit that reimburses approved coursework, its annual cap, and how to
claim it.

## Benefit

- Up to a fixed annual maximum for job-related coursework at an accredited
  institution.
- A passing grade is required for reimbursement; the claim is filed after
  grades post.

## Claiming

Claims are submitted with proof of payment and the final grade; approved claims
pay through the next expense reimbursement run.
