# Remote Work Policy

Who may work remotely, how often, and the approvals involved. Time off is
covered by the leave policy, not here.

## Eligibility

- Roles designated remote-eligible may work fully remote.
- Hybrid roles are expected on site two days per week by default.

## Approval

A change to a worker's remote designation is requested in Workday and approved
by the manager and HR business partner.
