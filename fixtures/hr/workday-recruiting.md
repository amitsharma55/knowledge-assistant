# Recruiting

How open positions are created, posted, and filled in Workday Recruiting.

## Job requisitions

- A hiring manager opens a requisition against a position; it routes for
  finance and HR approval before posting.
- Posted requisitions sync to the external careers site every hour.

## Candidate flow

Candidates move through screen, interview, offer, and hire stages. On hire, the
requisition closes and the new worker record is created for the start date.
