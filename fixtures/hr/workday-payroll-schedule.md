# Payroll Schedule

When pay periods open, close, and pay out. This is the calendar; the mechanics
of the payroll integration feed are documented separately.

## Cadence

- Salaried employees are paid semi-monthly, on the 15th and the last business
  day.
- Hourly employees are paid biweekly, one week in arrears.

## Cutoffs

Time and adjustments must be approved by 5pm two business days before pay date;
later changes fall to the next cycle as a retro.
