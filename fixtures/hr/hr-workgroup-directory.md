# HR Workgroup Directory

The support workgroup, single point of contact (SPOC), and how to reach the team
that owns HR systems and Workday operations. Use this when you need to raise or
route a ticket to the right group.

## Workgroup and contacts

- **Workgroup ID:** WG3333
- **Workgroup name:** HR Operations Support
- **Team:** HR (HR Operations team)
- **SPOC (single point of contact):** Dana Osei
- **SPOC email:** dana.osei@example.com
- **Support channel:** `#hr-operations`
- **Support hours:** Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00 local
- **Escalation:** payroll-affecting incidents escalate to the HR Operations
  manager the same business day

## Ticket routing

- Assign incidents and requests for HR systems and Workday to workgroup
  **WG3333**.
- The SPOC, Dana Osei, is the first contact for status, prioritization, and
  escalation of anything assigned to WG3333.
- Business-hours questions go to `#hr-operations`; payroll-affecting issues are
  escalated to the HR Operations manager.
