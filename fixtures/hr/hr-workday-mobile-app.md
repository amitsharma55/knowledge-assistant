# Workday Mobile App

What employees can do in the Workday mobile app.

## Capabilities

- View pay slips, request time off, and complete assigned tasks.
- Push notifications alert approvers to pending items.

## Security

The app requires device biometric unlock and is remotely wiped on termination.
