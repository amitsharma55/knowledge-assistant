# South Carolina Data Call

Filed with the South Carolina Department of Insurance. Shares the STAR pipeline
and the common data dictionary.

## Submission deadline

The South Carolina filing is due August 20.

## Distinguishing elements

South Carolina splits coastal exposure by the wind-and-hail zone the policy
falls in, reported with a `beach_zone_code`.
