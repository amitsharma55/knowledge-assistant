# Data Call Historical Archive

Where prior years' submitted packages and their filing receipts are retained
for audit and restatement.

## Retention

- Submitted packages are retained for ten years in cold storage.
- Each archived package keeps its filing receipt id and the checksum of the
  exact bytes delivered.

## Restatement

A restatement produces a new package version linked to the original; the
original is never overwritten.
