# Oklahoma Data Call

Filed with the Oklahoma Insurance Department. Oklahoma is an inland-peril call
focused on hail and tornado exposure rather than coastal wind.

## Submission deadline

The Oklahoma filing is due May 31.

## Distinguishing elements

Oklahoma requires a `hail_swath_zone` and reports tornado exposure in a
supplemental section separate from the base premium record.
