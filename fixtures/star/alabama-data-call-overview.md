# Alabama Data Call

Filed with the Alabama Department of Insurance. Reuses much of the Florida
package structure for coastal counties.

## Submission deadline

The Alabama filing is due June 30.

## Distinguishing elements

Alabama flags policies in the Alabama Beach Pool with an `alabama_beach_flag`,
reported separately from the voluntary book.
