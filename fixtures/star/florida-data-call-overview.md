# Florida Data Call

The Florida data call is filed with the Florida Office of Insurance Regulation
(FLOIR). It shares the STAR pipeline with the other states but has its own
scope, deadline, and reporting package.

## Scope

Residential and commercial property policies with Florida exposure, including
policies assumed from Citizens Property Insurance Corporation.

## Submission deadline

The FLOIR filing is due April 30 each year. A single extension of up to fifteen
days may be requested through the FLOIR portal before the original date.

## Distinguishing elements

Florida returns a `sinkhole_indicator` and an `fhcf_participation_flag` that no
other state's call carries; both are required on every property record.
