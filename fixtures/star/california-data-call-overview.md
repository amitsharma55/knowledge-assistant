# California Data Call

The California data call is filed with the California Department of Insurance
(CDI). It uses the STAR pipeline with a California-specific scope and schedule.

## Scope

Homeowners and dwelling-fire policies with California exposure.

## Submission deadline

The CDI filing is due September 30. Extensions are not granted routinely and
require a written hardship request.

## Distinguishing elements

California records carry a `brush_fire_zone` and an `earthquake_zone_code`.
Earthquake exposure is reported to CDI as a distinct supplemental package.
