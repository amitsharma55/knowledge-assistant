# Fraud Referral Feed

Sends suspected-fraud claim referrals from the claims system to the National
Insurance Crime Bureau (NICB) and the state fraud bureaus.

## Feed

- Referrals are transmitted nightly with the claim number, referral reason code,
  and adjuster narrative.
- A referral is never auto-created; an adjuster must flag the claim.
