# Star Workgroup Directory

The support workgroup, single point of contact (SPOC), and how to reach the team
that owns the Star data-call pipeline and statutory reporting. Use this when you
need to raise or route a ticket to the right group.

## Workgroup and contacts

- **Workgroup ID:** WG2222
- **Workgroup name:** Star Data Calls Support
- **Team:** Star (Statutory Data & Reporting team)
- **SPOC (single point of contact):** Marcus Lee
- **SPOC email:** marcus.lee@example.com
- **Support channel:** `#star-data-calls`
- **Support hours:** Monday–Friday, 07:00–19:00 ET
- **Escalation:** data-call submission-deadline incidents escalate to the team
  lead after 30 minutes

## Ticket routing

- Assign incidents and requests for Star data calls, validation, and
  submissions to workgroup **WG2222**.
- The SPOC, Marcus Lee, is the first contact for status, prioritization, and
  escalation of anything assigned to WG2222.
- Business-hours questions go to `#star-data-calls`; deadline-risk incidents are
  escalated to the team lead.
