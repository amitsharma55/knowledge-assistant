# MCAS Reporting

The Market Conduct Annual Statement (MCAS) reports claims and underwriting
market-conduct metrics to regulators through the NAIC. It is separate from the
state exposure data calls and runs on its own annual cycle.

## Metrics

- Claims closed with and without payment, median days to settle, and complaint
  counts by line of business.

## Filing

MCAS is filed once a year through the NAIC MCAS system, keyed by NAIC company
code, not by state portal.
