# NAIC Annual Statement

The statutory financial annual statement — the "yellow book" — filed with the
NAIC and every state of licensure. STAR supplies the statistical exhibits, not
the full financial statement.

## STAR's part

- STAR produces the state-page premium and loss exhibits that tie to the
  underwriting results.
- These exhibits reconcile to the same ledger control totals the data calls use.
