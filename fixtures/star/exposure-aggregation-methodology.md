# Exposure Aggregation Methodology

How insured exposure is rolled up for reporting, common to every state before
state-specific geographic splits apply.

## Method

- Exposure is aggregated at the policy level, then summed to the reporting
  geography.
- Multi-location policies are apportioned by insured value across locations.
